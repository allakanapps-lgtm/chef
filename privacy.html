Privacy Policy – Chef / شو نطبخ؟

Allakan Apps – contact: allakanapps@gmail.com

The app works fully offline. It does not collect, upload or share any personal data. Your pantry, plan, history and settings are stored only on your device and can be removed by clearing the app data or uninstalling.

The app has no ads, no analytics and no user accounts. Buttons such as "Ask Gemini" or WhatsApp only open the Gemini or WhatsApp app/site when you tap them, and only the text you choose to copy or send is shared.

Premium recipes are unlocked with an activation code that is checked locally on your device.
